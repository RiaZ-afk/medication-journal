Allopurinol

Chemical Formula:

C5H4N4O

Uses: Used to treat high uric acid concentrations in the blood and the conditions that result from it (gout, kidney stones).
How it Works: It blocks xanthine oxidase. Xanthine oxidase produces uric acid from purines.
Side Effects: Rashes, drowsiness, dizzines, nausea
Interesting Fact: It was originally intended to work alongside a chemotherapy drug.
